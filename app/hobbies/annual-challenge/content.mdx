## The Why

These challenges aren't about proving anything to anyone else. They're about:

- **Friendship** — shared suffering creates unbreakable bonds
- **Growth** — finding out what you're capable of when you commit
- **Stories** — creating memories worth telling
- **Fun** — because why not do something ridiculous with your spring break?

Every year, we ask ourselves: "What's next?" The answer is always something that sounds just slightly too ambitious — which makes it exactly right.
