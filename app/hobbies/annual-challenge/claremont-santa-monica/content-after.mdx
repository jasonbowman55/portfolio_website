## Reflections

Year two of the tradition proved that the best adventures come with zero training and complete commitment. What we learned:

- "Preparation" is overrated when you have good friends and determination
- LA County is much larger (and more varied) than it appears on a map
- Starting conditions don't matter — what matters is finishing together
- Some of the best experiences are the ones that sound slightly impossible at the start

This cemented our spring break tradition: every year, we ask ourselves "What's next?" and the answer is always something just ambitious enough to be worth documenting.
