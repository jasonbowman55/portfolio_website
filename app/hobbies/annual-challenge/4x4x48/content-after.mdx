## Reflections

This challenge became the foundation for our annual spring break tradition. What we learned:

- Sleep deprivation makes everything funnier
- Having a support system (even if they're waking you up at 3 AM) is crucial
- The hardest part isn't the physical challenge — it's staying committed when it stops being fun
- Great friends will do ridiculous things with you just because

This wasn't just about running — it was about commitment, friendship, and creating a story worth telling.
