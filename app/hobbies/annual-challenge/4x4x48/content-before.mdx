## Intro

Stemming from the challenge started by David Goggins called the 4x4x48 — run 4 miles, every 4 hours, for 48 hours — the video below highlights the journey of [Ket Hollingsworth](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ket-hollingsworth/) and [Collin Fitzpatrick](https://www.linkedin.com/in/collin-fitzpatrick-2155a9222/) doing this challenge, putting their own spin on it.

## The Plan

Run 4 miles every 4 hours for 48 hours, with the following 4-hour breakdown:

- **Hour 0–1:** Run 4 miles
- **Hour 1–3:** Learn something new / work on a project
- **Hour 3–4:** Sleep

## Situation / My Role

Collin and Ket, my roommates and close friends from day one at Harvey Mudd, wanted to take on something wild for spring break freshman year. We can't remember who first threw out the idea, but we were all instantly pumped to see how far we could take it. A mix of sleep deprivation, spending time together, and partaking in such a ridiculous task made for a very fun time. My job was to film and edit the final video, keep them on track, and come up with fun ways to wake them up every 4 hours. Good times.
