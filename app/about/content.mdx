This site started as a place to submit lab reports for E155: Microprocessor Design & Application at [Harvey Mudd College](https://www.hmc.edu/). It's since grown into a fuller portfolio — engineering projects, technical capabilities, and the hobbies that shape how I approach them. The goal is to bridge the professional and the personal: enough to show the work, and enough to show the person behind it.

I'm a hands-on engineer who believes in learning by building. Whether it's an embedded system for rocket stabilization, an autonomous robot, or a physical challenge with friends, I approach problems with curiosity, persistence, and a commitment to getting it right. This site is the record of that — the successes, the learning experiences, and the ongoing process of growth that ties the technical and personal together.
