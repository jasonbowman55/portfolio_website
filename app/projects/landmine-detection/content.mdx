My engineering capstone (Clinic) project, advised by [Professor Jason Gallicchio](https://www.hmc.edu/about/news/experts/jason-gallicchio/) and sponsored by Sokil. The project centers on drone-deployed sensor systems aimed at supporting humanitarian demining efforts in both civilian and battlefield settings, particularly in Ukraine — helping demining personnel rule out large unsuspected areas to make more efficient use of time and resources.

As team lead, I manage a six-person team through evaluating over ten sensor options and testing the three most promising: RADAR, LiDAR, and multispectral sensing. My own focus is on L/S band and mmWave RADAR systems, designing tests to detect PFM-1 landmines.

The project runs through May 2025, refining these sensor systems and integrating them onto a drone platform, with the goal of a system that can autonomously scan and flag suspected landmine-dense regions with high confidence.
