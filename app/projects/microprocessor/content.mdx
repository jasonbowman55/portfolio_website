E155: Microprocessor Design & Application — known on campus as [Micro Ps](https://hmc-e155.github.io/) — is a [Harvey Mudd College](https://www.hmc.edu/) course covering embedded systems design, ARM STM32 microcontroller programming in C, and FPGA development with SystemVerilog. Hands-on and project-based: GPIO programming, timing and interrupts, SPI/UART, digital signal processing, IoT connectivity, and cryptographic implementations, culminating in the [Squat Hero](/projects/squat-hero) capstone project.
