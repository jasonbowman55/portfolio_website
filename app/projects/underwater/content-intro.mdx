E80 is a course in the [Harvey Mudd College](https://www.hmc.edu/) Engineering Core program where students first work in pairs on foundational labs, then form teams of four for a final project. Our team investigated light intensity at depth — comparing GoPro footage as a baseline against custom photodiode sensor integrations.

We developed the electrical, mechanical, and software integrations needed to extract accurate RGB illuminance data at depths of up to 5 meters in Dana Point, CA, then compared it to the RGB-decomposed GoPro footage.
