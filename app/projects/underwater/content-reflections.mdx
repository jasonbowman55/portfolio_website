## Reflections

This project was one of my most formative experiences at Harvey Mudd. It combined technical challenges with team dynamics, requiring us to integrate electrical, mechanical, and software systems while working effectively as a team — and it taught me as much about clear communication and shared responsibility as it did about sensor integration. It laid the foundation for my continued interest in embedded systems, which I later carried into [Microprocessor Design](/projects/microprocessor) and [High-Power Rocketry](/projects/high-power-rocketry).
